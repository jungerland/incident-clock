# AMAZING HEALTH SYSTEM
# BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

**Business Associate:** ExampleCo, Inc. (Healthcare and Life Sciences)
**AHS Agreement No.** AHS-BAA-2025-0211
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is entered into by Amazing Health System, on behalf of itself and its affiliated hospitals and clinics ("Covered Entity"), and ExampleCo, Inc. ("Business Associate"). It applies to all agreements under which Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits Protected Health Information for or on behalf of Covered Entity (the "Underlying Agreements").

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Terms used but not defined in this BAA have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules (45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164), including "Breach," "Discovery," "Protected Health Information" ("PHI"), "Electronic Protected Health Information" ("ePHI") and "Unsecured Protected Health Information."

1.2 "Security Incident" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.304, limited to incidents involving ePHI that Business Associate or its Subcontractors create, receive, maintain or transmit for Covered Entity.

1.3 "Subcontractor" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

## 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

2.1 Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Underlying Agreements, as required by law, or as permitted by this BAA.

2.2 Business Associate shall limit its uses and disclosures of PHI to the minimum necessary.

2.3 Business Associate may de-identify PHI only in accordance with the safe harbor method at 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(b)(2), and may use the de-identified information only to provide and improve the services under the Underlying Agreements. Information de-identified in accordance with this Section 2.3 is not PHI and is not subject to this BAA.

2.4 A limited data set under 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(e) is PHI for all purposes of this BAA.

## 3. Safeguards

3.1 Business Associate shall comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with respect to ePHI.

3.2 Business Associate shall not store, process or access PHI outside the United States.

3.3 Business Associate shall maintain HITRUST r2 certification for the systems that store or process PHI.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and in no event later than ten (10) calendar days after Discovery.

4.2 Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Security Incident within five (5) business days after Business Associate becomes aware of it. This Section 4.2 serves as notice of, and no further report is required for, unsuccessful attempts such as pings, port scans and blocked log-in attempts that do not result in unauthorized access to ePHI.

4.3 Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA within ten (10) calendar days after becoming aware of it.

4.4 Reports under this Section 4 shall be made in writing to Covered Entity's Privacy Office at privacy-office@ahs.example, with a copy to its Information Security Office at iso@ahs.example.

4.5 A report of a Breach shall include, to the extent known, the identity of each individual affected, the PHI involved, the date of the Breach and of its Discovery, and the steps taken to mitigate it.

4.6 Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity's reasonable costs of notifying individuals, regulators and the media of a Breach caused by Business Associate or its Subcontractors.

## 5. Subcontractors

5.1 Business Associate shall ensure that each Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on its behalf agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to Business Associate under this BAA.

## 6. Individual Rights and Government Access

6.1 Business Associate shall make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity within ten (10) business days after Covered Entity's request, so Covered Entity can meet 45 C.F.R. § 164.524.

6.2 Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 On termination of the Underlying Agreements, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI within thirty (30) days. If return or destruction is not feasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this BAA to that PHI for as long as it retains it.
