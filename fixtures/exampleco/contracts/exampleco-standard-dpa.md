# ExampleCo, Inc. Data Processing Addendum

**Version 4.2. Effective July 1, 2025.**

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement or other written agreement between ExampleCo, Inc. ("ExampleCo") and the customer that accepts it ("Customer") (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data contained in Customer Data that ExampleCo processes on Customer's behalf in providing the Services.

1.2 "Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable Data Protection Laws.

1.3 "Personal Data Breach" means a breach of security of the Services that ExampleCo has confirmed led to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data. Personal Data Breach does not include unsuccessful attempts or activities that do not compromise the security of Customer Personal Data, including unsuccessful log-in attempts, pings, port scans, and denial-of-service attacks.

1.4 "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by ExampleCo that processes Customer Personal Data.

1.5 "Trust Center" means ExampleCo's public trust and security site at trust.exampleco.example.

1.6 "Usage Data" means aggregated or de-identified data about the use and performance of the Services that does not identify Customer or any natural person.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 This DPA applies only to ExampleCo's processing of Customer Personal Data. Customer is the controller and ExampleCo is the processor of Customer Personal Data.

2.2 ExampleCo will process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, which are set out in the Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's configuration of the Services.

## 3. Personnel

3.1 ExampleCo will ensure that personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by written obligations of confidentiality.

## 4. Security

4.1 ExampleCo will implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Annex 2 ("Security Measures"). ExampleCo may update the Security Measures from time to time, provided that updates do not materially reduce the overall security of the Services.

4.2 ExampleCo will maintain a SOC 2 Type II report and ISO/IEC 27001 certification for the Services, or successor standards that are substantially equivalent.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 Customer grants ExampleCo general authorization to engage Subprocessors. The current list of Subprocessors is published on the Trust Center.

5.2 ExampleCo will give notice of any new Subprocessor at least thirty (30) days before the new Subprocessor begins processing Customer Personal Data, by updating the Trust Center list and emailing Customers who have subscribed to Subprocessor updates on the Trust Center.

5.3 Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by written notice within fifteen (15) days after ExampleCo's notice. If the parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Services as its sole and exclusive remedy.

5.4 Where ExampleCo must replace a Subprocessor urgently to protect the security or continuity of the Services, ExampleCo may do so without prior notice and will notify Customer promptly afterward.

5.5 ExampleCo remains responsible for its Subprocessors' performance of the obligations in this DPA.

## 6. Data Location

6.1 Customer Personal Data will be stored at rest in the hosting region Customer selects for its Services.

6.2 ExampleCo personnel and Subprocessors may access Customer Personal Data from locations outside the selected hosting region to provide support, maintenance and security operations, including from ExampleCo's support center in Manila, Philippines. Any transfer is made under the transfer mechanism in Annex 3.

## 7. Personal Data Breach

7.1 ExampleCo will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after ExampleCo confirms a Personal Data Breach.

7.2 ExampleCo will send notice to the security contact Customer designates in the Services administration console. If no security contact is designated, ExampleCo will send notice to Customer's account owner.

7.3 ExampleCo's notice will describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the categories of data affected, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. ExampleCo will provide further information as it becomes available.

7.4 ExampleCo's notification of or response to a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence Features

8.1 ExampleCo will not use Customer Data to train or fine-tune any foundation model that is made available to other customers.

8.2 ExampleCo may use Usage Data to operate, secure and improve the Services.

8.3 ExampleCo lists the third-party model providers used by AI features as Subprocessors on the Trust Center. ExampleCo requires its model providers not to retain prompts or outputs beyond the time needed to return a response, except as required for abuse monitoring.

## 9. Audits and Testing

9.1 ExampleCo will make its most recent SOC 2 Type II report available to Customer through the Trust Center, subject to Customer's acceptance of the Trust Center non-disclosure terms.

9.2 ExampleCo will engage an independent third party to perform a penetration test of the Services at least annually and will make an executive summary of the results available through the Trust Center.

9.3 Customer agrees that the reports in Sections 9.1 and 9.2 satisfy any audit right under Data Protection Laws. Where a supervisory authority requires a further audit, Customer may conduct one on-site audit per twelve (12) month period, with at least sixty (60) days' written notice, during business hours, and at Customer's expense.

## 10. Deletion

10.1 Within ninety (90) days after termination or expiry of the Agreement, ExampleCo will delete Customer Personal Data from the Services. Backups are overwritten in the ordinary course within a further thirty-five (35) days.

10.2 ExampleCo will provide written certification of deletion upon Customer's written request.

## 11. Order of Precedence

11.1 If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA controls as to the processing of Customer Personal Data.

---

## Annex 2: Security Measures (summary)

- Customer Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication.
- ExampleCo maintains a business continuity and disaster recovery plan and tests it at least annually.

## Annex 3: Transfer Mechanism

Transfers of Customer Personal Data subject to EU or UK Data Protection Laws are made under the Standard Contractual Clauses, Module 2 or Module 3 as applicable.
