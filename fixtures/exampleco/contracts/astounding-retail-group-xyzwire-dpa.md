# ASTOUNDING RETAIL GROUP, INC.
# VENDOR DATA PROTECTION AGREEMENT

**Vendor:** XYZwire, Inc.
**Retailer Agreement No.** ARG-VDPA-2024-0093
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024

This Vendor Data Protection Agreement ("VDPA") is entered into by Astounding Retail Group, Inc. ("Retailer") and XYZwire, Inc. ("Vendor"), and forms part of the Subscription Agreement between them dated January 15, 2024 (the "Subscription Agreement").

---

## 1. Precedence

1.1 This VDPA prevails over the Subscription Agreement and over any data processing terms, policies or online terms of Vendor or its Affiliates.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

2.2 "Retailer Data" means all data that Retailer, its employees or its customers provide to Vendor or that Vendor collects from Retailer's systems, including telemetry, logs, traces and metrics, and all data derived from any of it.

2.3 "Data Incident" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, disclosure or loss of, Retailer Data, including while held by a Subprocessor.

2.4 "Subprocessor" means any third party, including any Vendor Affiliate, that processes Retailer Data on Vendor's behalf.

## 3. Processing

3.1 Vendor shall process Retailer Data only to provide the services described in the Subscription Agreement (the "Services") and on Retailer's written instructions.

3.2 Vendor shall not use Retailer Data to train or improve any machine learning model, except models used only to provide the Services to Retailer.

3.3 Vendor shall store Retailer Data only in the United States or Canada.

## 4. Security

4.1 Vendor shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards that meet or exceed the CIS Critical Security Controls, Implementation Group 2.

4.2 Vendor shall encrypt Retailer Data at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption.

4.3 Vendor shall obtain a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Services each year and provide it to Retailer within fifteen (15) days after Retailer's request.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 Vendor shall not permit any Subprocessor other than those listed in Appendix B to process Retailer Data without Retailer's prior written consent.

5.2 Vendor shall request consent for a new Subprocessor in writing at least thirty (30) days before the Subprocessor begins processing Retailer Data. Retailer's silence is not consent.

5.3 Vendor shall bind each Subprocessor in writing to obligations at least as protective as this VDPA, and remains liable for each Subprocessor's acts and omissions.

## 6. Data Incidents

6.1 Vendor shall notify Retailer of a Data Incident without undue delay and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after Vendor becomes aware of it.

6.2 Vendor shall send notice by email to Retailer Security Operations at secops@arg.example and by telephone to +1 (614) 555-0182. Notice to Retailer's account or procurement contacts does not satisfy Section 6.1.

6.3 Vendor's notice shall describe the Data Incident, the Retailer Data involved, any Subprocessor involved, and the steps Vendor has taken. Vendor shall supplement the notice as new information becomes available.

6.4 Vendor shall not inform Retailer's customers or any third party of a Data Incident involving Retailer Data without Retailer's prior written approval, except as required by law.

6.5 Vendor shall cooperate with Retailer's investigation and shall bear the reasonable costs of a Data Incident to the extent caused by Vendor or its Subprocessors.

## 7. Return and Deletion

7.1 Within thirty (30) days after the Subscription Agreement ends, Vendor shall delete all Retailer Data and confirm the deletion to Retailer in writing.

## 8. Audit

8.1 Retailer may audit Vendor's compliance with this VDPA once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice, and within ten (10) days after notice following a Data Incident.

## 9. Successors and Change of Control

9.1 This VDPA binds and benefits each party's successors and permitted assigns.

9.2 A change of control of Vendor, by merger, acquisition or otherwise, does not terminate or modify this VDPA. Any acquirer's standard terms do not apply to Retailer Data unless Retailer agrees in writing.
