Read the contract below and list every commitment in it.

A commitment is a term someone could track or be held to: something a party must do, must not do, or may do. It includes the other side's rights that put a duty on the vendor (such as audit or objection rights), and terms that decide which document or terms govern. Do not list definitions, recitals or general purpose text as commitments, but use the definitions when you read the rest of the contract: they often change what a commitment means.

List each distinct duty as its own item, even when two share a sentence or a section. Include exhibits, schedules and annexes.

When a baseline DPA is provided, compare each commitment with it and say how the contract differs. The contract you are extracting from is the only source of quotes. The baseline is for comparison only.
